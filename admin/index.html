Our Story

Your design. Pashvena craft.

The name comes from pashm, Kashmir's finest wool. At Pashvena, you're the designer. Send us a photo, a screenshot or just an idea: a shawl you saw on Instagram, a pheran your grandmother wore, a colour you can't find anywhere. Our designers and artisans in Kashmir bring it to life, by hand, exactly the way you want it.

Every piece is made with weaver and embroiderer families from Srinagar, so the artisans are paid fairly and the craft stays alive. Each one is made slowly, by hand, and no two are exactly alike.

— Mehvish
